# Final product state

The merchant experience uses Loyalty Card terminology and one coherent path: Library → Template Gallery → Card Builder → Loyalty Studio. Quick editing remains primary, English and Arabic content live in the same Builder, autosave remains active, and Change design preserves the existing card identity.

Studio retains the approved six areas: Overview, How it works, Customers & locations, Test, Launch, and Settings. Direct links now open each area without relying on prior client navigation. Draft, Ready to launch, Live, Paused, and Archived states continue to expose lifecycle-appropriate actions.

Studio read-only summaries resolve the same requested-locale customer content as Builder and customer previews. English remains the intentional fallback only when the requested localized value is absent; business and location names remain merchant-entered values.

Library cards render exact known visual data or a deliberate neutral summary. They do not invent colors, artwork, progress, or a conflicting stamp goal. Gallery cards progressively resolve through all 32 templates and preserve a usable mobile comparison layout.

Customer Web, Apple Wallet, and Google Wallet remain separate surfaces. Provider preview availability is not presented as provider production readiness. Publication does not promise an immediate refresh of an already installed Wallet pass.

P6 changed no backend endpoint, contract, database schema, economic rule, publication rule, or Flutter source.
