# W3 accessibility summary

The W3 accessibility project ran twice in Chromium and passed both times. It covers public enrollment, required/optional consent controls, English/Arabic direction, privacy, private-card and QR labeling, transfer controls, warning/error states, and unavailable Program behavior.

Automated axe checks reported no serious or critical violations in the exercised states. QR accessible labels describe purpose without exposing raw credentials. Browser logs are under `raw-test-output/`.
