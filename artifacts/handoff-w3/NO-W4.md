# No-W4 confirmation

W3 does not add a production loyalty ledger, production stamp issuance, reward redemption, staff scan endpoint, staff device pairing, offline staff operation, Flutter application, Smart Tap, NFC redemption, rotating barcode, or production enforcement of W4 spend/daily-limit policy.

The only progress created by W3 is the initial zeroed `MembershipProgressProjection`. Existing W2 Test Mode remains synthetic and isolated; it is not a customer operational API.

W4 ownership and invariants are documented in [../../docs/w3/w4-handoff.md](../../docs/w3/w4-handoff.md).
