# Accessibility summary

W4 dashboard surfaces use native controls, labelled inputs, focusable navigation, textual status,
empty/error/permission/upgrade states, English/Arabic copy and RTL inheritance. Analytics include
text and tables rather than chart-only meaning. Pairing exposes an accessible QR label without the
token.

Final automated results:

- W1/W2 accessibility: 3/3 passed.
- W3 public enrollment, RTL, privacy, transfer and unavailable states: 1/1 passed.
- W4 operational screens/dialogs and Arabic/Staff-denial states: 2/2 passed twice.
- Axe found no serious or critical violations in the asserted surfaces.
- Keyboard checks confirmed focus leaves the document body and dialogs expose semantic roles.

These automated checks supplement, but do not replace, manual screen-reader and real-device
testing before a public launch.
