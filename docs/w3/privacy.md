# W3 privacy

W3 collects only display name plus email when the published enrollment policy permits or requires it. Phone, birth date, address, password, and social login are not collected.

The customer sees Program content before the form and must separately accept Program terms and Waflo privacy disclosure. Marketing email consent is optional, off by default, and recorded independently.

Email is encrypted at rest and shown only in masked form where transfer requires it. It is not present in public discovery, QR payloads, Wallet public assets, provider-health responses, logs, or audit metadata.

The privacy page describes customer sessions, Wallet providers, secure transfer, retention boundaries, and the fact that legal effective dates remain subject to legal review.
