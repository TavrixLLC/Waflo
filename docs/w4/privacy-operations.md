# Privacy operations

Owner-only privacy export and erasure commands require explicit confirmation, a legal-basis or
reason string, idempotency, audit and background processing.

Privacy export produces a private encrypted customer package. Erasure revokes credentials,
sessions and public assets; queues Wallet invalidation; removes encrypted contacts and consent
payloads; and anonymizes the Customer. The append-only operational ledger remains with
non-identifying internal references because history cannot be rewritten.

Tavrix LLC/Waflo provides the platform; each merchant controls its operational customer purpose.
Retention periods, legal notices and jurisdiction-specific obligations require legal review.
