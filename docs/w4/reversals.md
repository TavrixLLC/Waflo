# Reversals and corrections

Staff may reverse their own eligible operation inside the pinned policy window. Managers use the
manager window and must provide a reason. Every reversal references the original entry and appends
the opposite semantic event.

A stamp reversal is blocked when it would invalidate a dependent reward. Final redemption reversal
is blocked after any new-cycle stamp event. Already-reversed operations cannot reverse twice.
Manual adjustments are Owner/Manager-only, reasoned, audited and risk-signalled. Positive
corrections unlock crossed rewards; negative corrections cannot invalidate an earned entitlement.
