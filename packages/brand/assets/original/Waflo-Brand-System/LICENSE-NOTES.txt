FONT & USAGE NOTES
==================

Recommended typefaces:
- Manrope: SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Noto Sans Arabic: SIL Open Font License 1.1

Font binaries are not bundled. Download the latest official versions from Google Fonts.
The outlined Waflo wordmark is a logo asset and does not depend on an installed font.

The Waflo logo and included templates are prepared for the Waflo project.
Before trademark registration, run a professional trademark similarity search in intended markets.
